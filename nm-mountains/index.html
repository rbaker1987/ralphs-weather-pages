Tranquil late-fall weather through Thanksgiving. Ski areas are opening, and winds pick up Friday and Saturday.

A ridge of high pressure keeps skies mostly sunny and winds light today and Thanksgiving, with temperatures near normal and some high cirrus. Nights stay very cold in the high valleys, with Angel Fire near 12 and Red River near 19 tonight. NWS Albuquerque expects breezy to windy conditions over the highlands Friday afternoon and stronger winds Saturday, with gusts possibly reaching 40 to 50 mph near the central mountain chain. Little or no precipitation is expected.

Cold nights

Calm, clear and very cold in the valleys

With light winds and mostly clear skies, cold air pools in the high valleys tonight and Thanksgiving night. NWS has Angel Fire near 12 tonight and 15 Thursday night, Red River near 19, and Taos near 21.

Mountain basins and canyon floors can run colder than forecast on nights like these.

Roads

Icy stretch on US-64 east of Taos

NMRoads lists US-64 east of Taos (mile markers 257 to 272) and NM-475 to Ski Santa Fe as snow packed and icy. I-40 around Santa Rosa was listed with wet and icy spots on Tuesday.

No closures are listed on the interstates or on NM-518, NM-434, NM-38, NM-75, NM-150 or NM-68.

Looking ahead

Windy weekend, mild early December

NWS Albuquerque expects windy days Saturday and again Monday with mostly dry shortwaves passing through. Temperatures stay near to above normal.

The Climate Prediction Center favors above-normal temperatures Nov 30 to Dec 8. El Niño is very strong, which tends to favor better snow odds in northern New Mexico as the winter goes on.
