Quiet Thanksgiving in the mountains. Strong winds arrive Saturday, and a wetter storm may follow midweek.

Sunny skies and light winds continue today with seasonal temperatures and some high cirrus. Angel Fire dropped to about 13 this morning, and the high valleys stay very cold tonight. Winds pick up Friday afternoon over the central highlands, then a fast-moving system brings strong northwest winds Saturday. NWS Albuquerque says highland gusts could reach 45 to 60 mph and a High Wind Watch may be issued. Another breezy day comes Monday, and a Pacific storm could bring rain and snow to western and central areas around Wednesday.

Saturday

Strong winds over the highlands

A fast-moving system dives out of the northern Rockies Saturday. NWS Albuquerque expects the strongest winds near and just east of the central mountain chain in the afternoon, with gusts of 45 to 60 mph possible.

Winds like that can make driving difficult for high-profile vehicles and can damage weak trees and utility poles. Winds ease in the west Saturday evening but stay strong in the northeast past midnight.

Roads

Icy stretch still listed on US-64

NMRoads still lists US-64 east of Taos (mile markers 257 to 272) as snow packed and icy, last updated Tuesday. No severe driving conditions are listed anywhere in the state.

Nothing is listed for the interstates or for NM-518, NM-434, NM-38, NM-75, NM-150 or NM-68.

Looking ahead

Wetter pattern next week

NWS Albuquerque says a deeper Pacific low could bring rain and snow to parts of western and central New Mexico around Wednesday.

The Climate Prediction Center favors wetter than normal conditions around the Four Corners Dec 3 to 9. El Niño is very strong, which tends to favor better snow odds in northern New Mexico as winter goes on.
