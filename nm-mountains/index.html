Snow today

A quick round of mountain snow

NWS Albuquerque says the strongest lift reaches north-central New Mexico early this afternoon, with a brief period of heavy snow in the mountains. West and southwest slopes of the Sangre de Cristos are favored, with localized amounts up to about 6 inches on the peaks.

Between 6,000 and 8,000 feet it will be a rain and snow mix. Snow levels run lower on the east side, where Las Vegas and Santa Rosa could see flakes but no more than an inch.

Roads

Slick spots on US-64 and NM-38

NMRoads lists US-64 from east of Taos to west of Eagle Nest and NM-38 from Questa to Red River as snow packed and icy. NM-518, NM-434 and the interstates had no reported issues early this morning.

Expect slick travel above 8,000 feet this afternoon into Tuesday morning. Check nmroads.com before heading out.

Looking ahead

Dry week, then a more active pattern

A ridge builds Tuesday and Wednesday, keeping winds light and nights very cold in the high valleys. Dry northwest flow continues through Saturday.

NWS Albuquerque says a stronger Pacific jet may bring a more active storm track in the first week of December, though warmer air with it would mean high snow levels. El Niño remains strong to very strong.
