Cold front

Coldest night of the season Saturday

NWS Albuquerque says Saturday's front may be the strongest of the season so far for temperature change, dropping temps 10 to 20 degrees. Expect teens and 20s across the northern mountains Saturday night, with colder readings in high valleys like the Moreno Valley. Angel Fire already reached about 12° this morning. Protect outdoor pipes and pets.

Roads & fire

Wet spots on two mountain routes

NMRoads lists US-64 through Taos Canyon and NM-38 between Questa and Red River as difficult, with wet roads that may be slick. No closures on NM-518, NM-434, NM-75 or the interstates as of this morning.

No critical fire weather is expected.

This winter

Strong El Niño favors a snowier season

El Niño is strong and still strengthening. It usually sends more storms across the southern Rockies, which improves the odds for snowfall in the northern New Mexico mountains. It doesn't guarantee a big season, but the odds lean our way.
