Mild and mostly sunny today. Strong winds Saturday, a cold Saturday night, then a chance of rain and snow midweek.

High clouds and mild temperatures today, running 5 to 15 degrees above normal, with afternoon breezes over the central highlands. Winds strengthen on peaks and ridges tonight. A fast-moving system brings strong winds Saturday afternoon, with gusts of 35 to 55 mph, highest over the central highlands and the central mountain chain. NWS Albuquerque has High Wind Watches for the central highlands and Union County. Saturday night turns colder, with lows in the teens in the high valleys. Saturday

Strong winds over the highlands

A fast-moving system crosses northern New Mexico Saturday while colder air pushes in from the northeast. NWS Albuquerque expects gusts of 35 to 55 mph Saturday afternoon, highest over the central highlands and the central mountain chain.

Winds stay strong in the northeast through Saturday night. Expect difficult driving for high-profile vehicles, and some weak trees could come down.

Cold nights

Teens in the high valleys

Skies clear and winds ease Saturday night. NWS has Angel Fire near 13 and Red River near 18, and enclosed valleys could run colder than forecast if winds go calm.

Angel Fire dropped to about 17 this morning. Protect exposed pipes in unheated buildings.

Looking ahead

Pacific storm midweek

NWS Albuquerque expects a Pacific low to bring measurable rain and snow to much of western and central New Mexico Wednesday and Thursday, with lighter amounts east of the mountains.

The Climate Prediction Center favors wetter than normal conditions for the Southwest Dec 2 to 10. El Niño is very strong, which tends to favor better snow odds in northern New Mexico as winter goes on.
