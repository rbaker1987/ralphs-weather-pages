Monday's snow was light. Icy spots linger on mountain roads this morning before a dry, calm stretch.

Monday's storm left about an inch of new snow at the high SNOTEL sites. Personal weather stations showed the mid teens in the coldest high valleys to near 30 around Taos and Española at sunrise. NWS Albuquerque warns of slick spots on northern mountain roads and patchy fog early this morning. Dry, tranquil weather holds through Thanksgiving, with winds picking up this weekend.

Cold nights

Dry and calm, with very cold valley nights

High pressure builds today and Wednesday, keeping winds light and skies mostly clear. NWS has Angel Fire near 10 tonight and in the low teens Wednesday night, with Red River around 18 to 22.

Thanksgiving looks dry with some high clouds as a weak low passes well to the south.

Roads & fire

Icy spots this morning

NMRoads lists US-64 between Taos and Eagle Nest and NM-475 to Ski Santa Fe as snow packed and icy. I-25 near Raton Pass, Wagon Mound, Las Vegas and Rowe, and I-40 around Santa Rosa and Tijeras, have wet roads with icy spots. No closures are listed on the interstates.

Fire weather stays below critical levels. NWS notes stronger gusts this weekend as late-fall grasses keep drying.

Looking ahead

Windier weekend, then watching December

NWS Albuquerque expects breezy to windy days from Friday into early next week, with little or no precipitation and highs 5 to 10 degrees above normal.

The Climate Prediction Center leans above normal for both temperature and precipitation in New Mexico from Nov 29 to Dec 7, with about average confidence. The systems NWS can see so far look mostly dry.
