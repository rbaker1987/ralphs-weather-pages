Snow and cold

Coldest morning so far, more snow Monday

NWS Albuquerque says lows this morning reached the teens and 20s across central and northern New Mexico, the coldest of the season. Angel Fire fell to about 10 degrees under clear, calm skies.

A neutrally tilted trough reaches the southern Rockies Monday and pulls in some Gulf moisture. Expect rain and mountain snow showers Monday afternoon into Tuesday morning, mainly in the northern half of the state, with a few inches above 9,000 feet.

Roads & fire

Slick spots on US-64 near Eagle Nest

NMRoads listed US-64 from east of Taos to west of Eagle Nest as snow packed and icy after Saturday's snow, along with NM-475 and parts of US-64 near Tierra Amarilla. I-25 at Raton Pass had wet and icy spots.

Monday night's snow showers can make passes and shaded canyons slick again. No fire weather concerns this week.

Looking ahead

Dry after Tuesday, warming late week

NWS Albuquerque expects dry northwest flow from Tuesday night through the weekend, with temperatures rising a few degrees above normal by late week. The Climate Prediction Center favors above-normal temperatures for New Mexico into early December.

El Niño is very strong. It often sends more storms across the southern Rockies later in winter, though it does not guarantee a snowy season.
