Cold front

Strongest front of the season Saturday

NWS Albuquerque says Saturday's Pacific front will drop temperatures 10 to 20 degrees. It is short on moisture, so snow will be brief. Flurries could reach as low as 5,500 ft, with any minor accumulation, under an inch, mostly in the northern and western high terrain.

Saturday night lows fall into the teens and 20s in most areas. NWS forecasts about 5 to 12 degrees at Angel Fire and near 10 at Red River. NWS suggests winterizing homes now; protect outdoor pipes and pets.

Roads & fire

Mountain roads in good shape this morning

NMRoads lists no weather problems on NM-518, US-64, NM-434, NM-38, NM-75, NM-150 or NM-68. Yesterday's wet spots on US-64 in Taos Canyon and NM-38 have cleared. Expect road work on NM-434 south of Black Lake (MM 21–24).

Saturday's brief snow could leave slick spots on passes and in shaded canyons, and the cold Saturday night will refreeze anything wet.

NWS expects no critical fire weather for the next seven days. Fuels remain very wet.

This winter

Strong El Niño favors a snowier season

El Niño is strong and still strengthening. It usually sends more storms across the southern Rockies, which improves the odds for snowfall in the northern New Mexico mountains. It doesn't guarantee a big season, but the odds lean our way.
