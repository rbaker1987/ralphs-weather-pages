Moreno Valley

Angel Fire could hit 10° Wednesday night

Fresh snow, clear skies and calm wind let cold air pool in high basins like the Moreno Valley. Angel Fire is forecast near 10°F, and other sheltered valleys can run well below forecast too. Protect outdoor pipes and pets.

Roads & fire

Mountain routes clear for now

NMRoads shows no closures or difficult conditions on NM-518, US-64, NM-434, NM-75, NM-38 or I-25 near Las Vegas as of this morning. Watch for slick spots on the passes early Wednesday.

No critical fire weather is expected for at least 7 days.

This winter

Strong El Niño favors a snowier season

El Niño is strong and still strengthening. It usually sends more storms across the southern Rockies, which improves the odds for snowfall in the northern New Mexico mountains. It doesn't guarantee a big season, but the odds lean our way.
