Thanksgiving week

Wetter and cooler, but no Arctic air

NWS Shreveport and Fort Worth both point to a pattern change early next week. A slow storm system over the Gulf Coast could bring a soaking rain Mon–Tue, then cooler days through the holiday. The Climate Prediction Center favors above-normal rain for Texas Nov 22–26.

Freezing nights look unlikely through the end of November.

This winter

A strong El Niño is in charge

El Niño is now strong and still strengthening, near +2.5°C in the key Pacific region. That usually means an active southern storm track, with more rain and more cool, cloudy days in East Texas.

It does not bring Arctic air on its own. Ice or snow still needs cold air from Canada, and Canada is running warm right now.
