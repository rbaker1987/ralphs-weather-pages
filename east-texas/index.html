Thanksgiving week

Cooler, with a rain chance midweek

NWS Shreveport expects temperatures near or a little below late-November normals after Sunday's front. A second front Tuesday into Wednesday may bring rain, and Thanksgiving Day looks dry for now.

The Climate Prediction Center gives better than even odds of below-normal temperatures for much of central and eastern Texas Nov 25–29. For Nov 27 – Dec 3 it leans cool and wetter than normal from the southern Plains to the Gulf Coast, with low confidence.

This winter

A strong El Niño is in charge

El Niño is now strong and still strengthening, near +2.5°C in the key Pacific region. That usually means an active southern storm track, with more rain and more cool, cloudy days in East Texas.

It does not bring Arctic air on its own. Ice or snow still needs cold air from Canada, and Canada is running warm right now.
