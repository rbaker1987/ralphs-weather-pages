Thanksgiving week

Cooler, but not Arctic

After Sunday's front, NWS Shreveport expects temperatures near late-November normals, perhaps slightly below by Tuesday. The Climate Prediction Center leans below normal for the Southern Plains Nov 24 – Dec 2.

Rain looks limited through the holiday. Better rain chances may come just after Thanksgiving, but details are uncertain. Some single model runs show a big storm that weekend; most guidance does not support that yet.

This winter

A strong El Niño is in charge

El Niño is now strong and still strengthening, near +2.5°C in the key Pacific region. That usually means an active southern storm track, with more rain and more cool, cloudy days in East Texas.

It does not bring Arctic air on its own. Ice or snow still needs cold air from Canada, and Canada is running warm right now.
