Why rural low spots freeze first

On clear, calm nights the ground loses heat straight to space, and the air touching it cools fastest. That cold air is heavier, so it slides downhill and pools in creek bottoms, river valleys and open sandy fields. Those spots can run 5 to 8 degrees colder than town, where buildings and pavement hold heat.

That is why NWS Shreveport has about 32 for Tyler tonight while rural low spots fall into the mid to upper 20s. A Freeze Watch runs until 9 AM Thursday, and Friday morning looks similar. Cover tender plants, bring pets in, and protect exposed outdoor faucets.

Looking ahead

Warm and dry into early December

The Climate Prediction Center strongly favors above-normal temperatures for Texas from Nov 30 to Dec 8, with near to below normal rain for much of the state. Highs in the upper 60s to 70s look likely next week.

Some European ensemble members show a better rain chance around Dec 6 to 8, but that is still far out and confidence is low. El Niño remains very strong, which tends to favor a wetter pattern later in the winter.
