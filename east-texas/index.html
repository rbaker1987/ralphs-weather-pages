Thanksgiving week

Colder, with several frosty mornings

NWS Shreveport says temperatures return to late-November normals Monday, then a stronger system Tuesday night reinforces the cold air. It calls consecutive sub-freezing mornings very probable across northern parts of the region from Wednesday through Thanksgiving and beyond. Around Tyler, lows in the low to mid 30s are most likely, colder in rural low spots.

The Climate Prediction Center favors below-normal temperatures in East Texas for Nov 26–30, then a warmer pattern for most of the country Nov 28 – Dec 4.

This winter

A very strong El Niño

The Climate Prediction Center says El Niño is strong to very strong, near +2.5°C in the key Pacific region, with a chance of a record event this winter.

NWS Fort Worth notes that El Niño has little effect on North Texas weather until after the New Year, and a strongly negative Pacific Decadal Oscillation may keep this winter drier than a typical El Niño.
