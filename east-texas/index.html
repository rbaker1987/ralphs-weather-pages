Thanksgiving week

Turning colder, with a freeze possible

NWS Shreveport says temperatures return to normal Monday, then a second front Tuesday night brings colder air through the holiday. It expects freezing temperatures in many areas on Thanksgiving morning and again on Black Friday. Any rain with that front looks light and brief, and Thanksgiving Day looks dry for now.

The Climate Prediction Center favors below-normal temperatures from eastern and southern Texas to the Gulf Coast for Nov 26–30, with wetter than normal odds near the Gulf. For Nov 28 – Dec 4 it leans near normal for temperature and wetter than normal for southern and eastern Texas.

This winter

A strong El Niño is in charge

The Climate Prediction Center says El Niño is strong to very strong and still strengthening, near +2.5°C in the key Pacific region. That usually means an active southern storm track and more cool, cloudy, wet days in East Texas.

It does not bring Arctic air on its own. Ice or snow still needs cold air from Canada.
