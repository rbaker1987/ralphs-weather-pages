Thanksgiving week

Cold mornings, sunny afternoons

NWS Shreveport says a second, stronger disturbance dives through Tuesday night with patchy light rain, mainly over southern and eastern areas. It then reinforces the cold air, with much of the region near or below freezing Wednesday through Friday mornings.

Thanksgiving Day looks dry and sunny with highs in the upper 50s. Temperatures return to near normal this weekend as south winds come back.

Looking ahead

Warmer and wetter into early December

The Climate Prediction Center favors above-normal temperatures and above-normal rain for Texas from Nov 29 to Dec 5, with low confidence. Weather models show highs in the 70s around Dec 1 and a chance of rain near Dec 3.

El Niño remains strong to very strong. It tends to favor an active southern storm track this winter.
