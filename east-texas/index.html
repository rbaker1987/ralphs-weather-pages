This morning

The first widespread freeze

Clear skies and light winds let temperatures drop to about 33 in Tyler this morning, with rural low spots in the 20s. NWS Shreveport had a Freeze Warning out for Smith County and much of the region until 9 AM.

Friday morning looks similar but a degree or two milder in town. After that, south winds return and lows climb into the 40s and 50s for the weekend.

Looking ahead

Warm early December, rain chance returns

The Climate Prediction Center favors above-normal temperatures for Texas through Dec 9 and leans wetter than normal in the Dec 3 to 9 period. The European and American models both show a rain system around Dec 3 to 6, though they disagree on timing and amounts.

It is still a week or more out, so confidence is moderate. El Niño remains very strong, which tends to favor a more active storm track later in the winter.
